# AMT Question Banks

Free practice questions for aviation maintenance (AMT) students. Pick a bank, answer, and see right away whether you got it.

**Open the site**

- Main site: https://question-bank-78u.pages.dev
- Mirror (same questions, backup link): https://shicheng0810.github.io/question-bank/

**What is in it**

Banks are grouped by category and term:

- **General** — one big bank that covers every General course
- **Airframe** — Term 1, 2, 3
- **Powerplant** — Term 1, 2, 3 (added as the courses start)

**What you can do**

- **Practice without an account.** Open a bank and start. Your progress is saved in your browser.
- **Sign in with a code to keep history across devices.** Pick a personal code, enter the same code on another phone or laptop, and your practice history comes with you.
- **Answer sheet.** Jump to any question, see which ones you got right, wrong, or starred.
- **Wrong and starred review.** Questions you missed or starred are collected so you can redo just those.
- **Import your own bank with an AI.** Give your notes, screenshots, or a PDF to an AI chat, ask it to write the questions in the site's format, and paste the result into the home page. The format and a ready-made prompt are here: https://question-bank-78u.pages.dev/format.html
- **Feedback.** Found a wrong answer or a typo? Use the feedback button inside any bank.

The questions are study practice. They are not an official exam and not a substitute for your instructor's materials.

# AMT 题库

给航空维修（AMT）学生的免费练习题。选一个题库，做题，马上知道对错。

**打开网站**

- 主站：https://question-bank-78u.pages.dev
- 镜像（题目相同，备用链接）：https://shicheng0810.github.io/question-bank/

**有什么**

题库按分类和学期整理：

- **General** — 一个大题库，覆盖 General 的所有课程
- **Airframe** — 学期 1、2、3
- **Powerplant** — 学期 1、2、3（随开课陆续加入）

**能做什么**

- **不用账号直接练习。** 打开题库就能做，进度保存在你的浏览器里。
- **用登录码跨设备保留记录。** 领一次码，在另一台手机或电脑上输入，练习历史就跟着过去。
- **答题卡。** 一眼看到每道题的状态，可以跳到任意一题。
- **错题 / 收藏复习。** 做错的和收藏的题会自动收集，可以只重做这些。
- **用 AI 导入你自己的题库。** 把笔记、截图或 PDF 交给 AI，让它按网站的格式出题，再粘贴到首页导入。格式说明和现成的提示词在这里：https://question-bank-78u.pages.dev/format.html
- **反馈。** 发现答案错了或有错别字？在任意题库里点反馈按钮告诉我们。

这些题只用于学习练习，不是官方考试，也不能代替老师给的资料。
